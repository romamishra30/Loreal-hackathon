# RefillRx - 3-Minute Competition Demo Script
**L'Oréal Sustainability Challenge 2026 • Medical Track**  
*Case: Engaging Dispensing Dermatologists*

---

## Pitch Narrative Overview (3 Minutes Total)

This presentation script guides you through demonstrating the working platform, directly addressing the jury's key deliverables **(a)**, **(b)**, **(c)**, and **(d)** in under 180 seconds.

---

### Phase 1: Context & Deliverable (a) – Understand the Doctor (0:00 – 0:45)
**Screen to show**: `Landing Page` (`/`) → Click **"3-Min Demo Tour"** or Navigate to **"Understand Doctor"** (`/doctor-matrix`).

* **Voiceover**:
  > "Distinguished judges, 45% of dermatologists in key markets are *dispensing dermatologists*. They run hybrid practices stocking skincare directly in their clinics. 
  > 
  > Purely prescribing doctors only ask: *'Is it clinically proven?'* But dispensing doctors think like small-business owners: *'What is my gross margin? Will unsold refill inventory tie up working capital? Will this bottleneck my reception staff?'*
  > 
  > In our **Prescriber vs. Dispenser Matrix**, we map these 8 critical decision drivers side-by-side. Furthermore, our **8-Question Diagnostic Quiz** enables medical reps to classify each doctor into 5 archetypes—such as the *Margin-Driven Entrepreneur* or *Clinician-First Purist*—automatically tuning the pitch angle and anticipating objections before stepping into the clinic."

---

### Phase 2: Deliverable (c) – Pitch Studio & Economics (0:45 – 1:30)
**Screen to show**: Top Navigation → **"Pitch & Economics Studio"** (`/pitch-studio`).

* **Voiceover**:
  > "To make dispensing dermatologists prioritize our refill range over high-margin local competitors, we pitch refill not as charity, but as a **Practice-Growth & Patient-Retention engine**.
  > 
  > In the **Live Economics Calculator**, adjusting patient footfall and repeat rates shows that while full packs earn 25% margin, refills earn **32% margin**. More importantly, patient repeat adherence jumps from 28% to 62%—generating over **₹1.4 Lakhs in net incremental practice profit annually**, net of all staff handling costs.
  > 
  > We completely eliminate working capital risk through a **100% Sale-or-Return stock guarantee**. Our **Objection Library** equips reps with 3-part answers (*Acknowledge → Evidence → Commercial Offer*) for 12 tough doctor concerns, alongside ready-to-print **Green Clinic Certificates** that turn sustainability into a clinic prestige asset."

---

### Phase 3: Deliverable (b) – Clinic Refill Counter & 3-Tap Flow (1:30 – 2:15)
**Screen to show**: Role Switcher in Top Bar → Select **"Clinic Staff / Dispenser"** → **Refill Counter** (`/counter`).

* **Voiceover**:
  > "In-clinic execution fails if reception staff are burdened. We designed the **3-Tap Clinic Counter** to execute in under **25 seconds with zero training**:
  > 
  > 1. **Tap 1**: Tap *Simulate Scan*—the patient's QR pass is recognized in 2 seconds, displaying their 20% circular discount and usage history.
  > 2. **Tap 2**: Quick 5-second check of container integrity and lot code. Our real-time anti-fraud algorithm rejects duplicate batch codes, preventing unauthorized reuse.
  > 3. **Tap 3**: Select payment mode and tap *Confirm Dispense*.
  > 
  > Instantly, clinic inventory decrements, the under-counter returns bin updates toward its 20-bottle threshold, staff earn reward points, and the transaction logs offline or online. When the returns bin is full, one tap schedules courier collection for PCR recycling."

---

### Phase 4: Deliverable (d) – Territory Dashboard & Adoption Health Score (2:15 – 3:00)
**Screen to show**: Role Switcher → Select **"Medical Rep"** → **Territory Dashboard** (`/territory`).

* **Voiceover**:
  > "How does the Brand know execution is consistent across the territory? 
  > 
  > In our **Territory Dashboard**, Rep Vikram monitors 25 dispensing clinics across Mumbai–Thane. Every clinic receives an **Adoption Health Score (0–100)** evaluating refill rates (40%), weekly consistency (25%), stock cover (20%), and staff engagement (15%).
  > 
  > Clinics are categorized as *Champions*, *On Track*, *At Risk*, or *Dormant*. The **Action Centre** automatically detects anomalies—such as a clinic with zero refills for 10 days—prioritizing weekly rep coaching routes.
  > 
  > Finally, in our **Brand Executive View**, regional directors can inspect multi-territory rollups and use the **National Scale Simulator** to project 1,500 clinics diverting over 60,000 kg of virgin plastic annually. RefillRx turns dispensing dermatologists from passive prescribers into active circular partners."
